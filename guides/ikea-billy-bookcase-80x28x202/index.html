How to assemble the IKEA BILLY Bookcase 80x28x202 cm

Tools you need

  • Phillips screwdriver
  • Flat screwdriver
  • Soft surface to work on (rug or blanket)
  • Drill for the wall holes (not shown in the manual)

What's in the box

Parts

  • 1×Side panel
  • 1×Side panel
  • 3×Board (top, bottom and fixed shelf)
  • 1×Plinth
  • 1×Back panel (fibreboard, folded)
  • 1×Back panel (fibreboard, folded)
  • 4×Loose shelf

Hardware

  • 12×Cam bolt
  • 16×Wooden dowel
  • 18×Push pin for the back panel
  • 18×Plug for the back panel
  • 12×Cam lock
  • 16×Shelf support
  • 2×Screw with washer for the bracket
  • 2×Wall bracket
  • 2×Wall screw with washer 100823 (screw not included)Not included
  • 2×Wall plugNot included

Step by step

  1. 1

    Step 1: Dowels into the boards

    Tap the 16 wooden dowels into the holes in the ends of the three boards and the plinth.

    0:23
  2. 2

    Step 2: Cam bolts into the sides

    Lay both side panels on a rug, inner face up, and screw six cam bolts into each one.

    Soft surface to work on (rug or blanket)Phillips screwdriver
    0:34
  3. 3

    Step 3: Boards onto the first side

    Stand the plinth, the bottom board and the fixed shelf on one side panel, with their dowels in its holes.

    1:02
  4. 4

    Step 4: Lock the boards with cam locks

    Push four cam locks into the holes and turn them clockwise with a flat screwdriver to lock them.

    Flat screwdriver
    1:19
  5. 5

    Step 5: Put on the top board

    Put the top board on the side and lock it with two cam locks.

    Flat screwdriver
    1:30
  6. 6

    Step 6: Put on the second side

    Turn the second side over and lower it onto the dowels and cam bolts.

    1:48
  7. 7

    Step 7: Lock the second side

    Lock the second side with the other six cam locks.

    Flat screwdriver
    2:05
  8. 8

    Step 8: Slide in the back panel

    Turn the bookcase onto its front.

    2:18
  9. 9

    Step 9: Slide in the back panel

    Unfold the back panel and slide it into the grooves from the bottom.

    2:18
  10. 10

    Step 10: Fix the back panel

    Fix the back panel with 18 push fasteners: push a plug through each hole, then press a pin into it.

    2:40
  11. 11

    Step 11: Fix the bookcase to the wall

    Stand the bookcase up and push it against the wall.

    Safety: Always fix the bookcase to the wall: it can tip over and cause serious injuries.

    2:54
  12. 12

    Step 12: Fix the bookcase to the wall

    Screw a wall bracket onto the top at the back on each side, with a screw and washer.

    Phillips screwdriver
    2:54
  13. 13

    Step 13: Fix the bookcase to the wall

    Drill a hole for each bracket, push in a plug and fix the bracket with a screw and washer.

    Drill for the wall holes (not shown in the manual)Phillips screwdriver

    Note: Wall screws and plugs are not included: choose ones that suit your wall.

    2:54
  14. 14

    Step 14: Push in the shelf supports

    Push the 16 shelf supports into the holes at the heights you want.

    3:26
  15. 15

    Step 15: Lay in the shelves

    Lay the four shelves on the supports.

    3:36
