How to assemble the IKEA KALLAX Shelving unit 77x39x77 cm

Tools you need

  • Allen key 100001In the box
  • Phillips screwdriver
  • Flat screwdriver
  • Pencil
  • Soft surface to work on (rug or blanket)
  • Drill for the wall holes (not shown in the manual)

What's in the box

Parts

  • 1×Outer panel with screw holes, top (A1)
  • 1×Outer panel with screw holes, bottom (A2)
  • 2×Outer panel with dowel holes, side (B1, B2)
  • 1×Inner panel, long (middle shelf)
  • 2×Inner panel, short (upright divider)

Hardware

  • 8×Long screw, hex socket
  • 10×Wooden dowel
  • 4×Short screw for the angle bracket
  • 2×Angle bracket for wall fixing
  • 2×Bracket cover
  • 2×Keyhole plate
  • 2×Round-head wall screwNot included
  • 2×Wall plugNot included

Step by step

  1. 1

    Step 1: Lay the top and a side on their back edges on a rug. Join them with two long screws and the Allen key.

    Soft surface to work on (rug or blanket)Allen key 100001
    0:23
  2. 2

    Step 2: Tap two dowels into the middle of the top's inner face and push a short inner panel onto them.

    0:36
  3. 3

    Step 3: Tap two dowels into the middle of the side and slide the long inner panel onto them.

    0:46
  4. 4

    Step 4: Tap two dowels into the middle of the long panel's free face.

    0:56
  5. 5

    Step 5: Push the second short inner panel onto these dowels.

    1:06
  6. 6

    Step 6: Tap two dowels into the free end of the long panel and two into the free end of the short panel.

    1:16
  7. 7

    Step 7: Push the second side onto the two dowels of the long panel.

    1:26
  8. 8

    Step 8: Put the bottom panel on, onto the dowels of the short panel.

    1:36
  9. 9

    Step 9: Fix the corners with long screws

    Fix both bottom corners with four long screws through the bottom panel and the Allen key.

    Allen key 100001
    1:46
  10. 10

    Step 10: Fix the last corner with two long screws through the top into the second side.

    Allen key 100001
    1:58
  11. 11

    Step 11: Stand the shelving unit upright.

    2:08
  12. 12

    Step 12: Screw an angle bracket to the inside of each side, at the back just under the top, with two screws each.

    Phillips screwdriver
    2:18
  13. 13

    Step 13: Mark the wall

    Hold the unit against the wall at the height you want and mark the wall through each bracket hole with a pencil.

    Pencil

    Safety: Lift the unit with two people. The wall and the screws must carry its full weight.

    2:31
  14. 14

    Step 14: Drive the wall screws

    Take the unit down and drive a round-head screw into each mark, leaving the head out.

    Drill for the wall holes (not shown in the manual)Flat screwdriver

    Note: Wall screws and plugs are not included. Use round-head screws that suit your wall.

    2:41
  15. 15

    Step 15: Hang the unit on the wall

    Hang the unit on the screws, put a keyhole plate on each bracket over the screw head, tighten the screw and push on the cover.

    Flat screwdriver
    2:56
