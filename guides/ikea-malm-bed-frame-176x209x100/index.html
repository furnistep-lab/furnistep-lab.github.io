Step by step

  1. 1

    Step 1: Glides into the boards

    Lay the lower headboard and the footboard on a rug and tap three glides into the bottom edge of each.

    Soft surface to work on (rug or blanket)Hammer
    0:24
  2. 2

    Step 2: Cam bolts into the boards

    Screw four cam bolts into the lower headboard: into the top and the bottom hole of the three at each end.

    Wrench

    Safety: Stop turning when the collar of the bolt touches the board.

    0:41
  3. 3

    Step 3: Cam bolts into the boards

    Screw four cam bolts into the inner face of the footboard the same way.

    Wrench
    0:41
  4. 4

    Step 4: Stand up the frame parts

    Stand the two boards up, facing each other.

    1:05
  5. 5

    Step 5: Stand up the frame parts

    Stand the side rails between them, with a gap at each end of the rails.

    1:05
  6. 6

    Step 6: Dowels into the side rails

    Push three short dowels into the end of the side rail: two into the top, one into the middle hole.

    Safety: Use the 12 short dowels here, not the 5 long ones.

    1:28
  7. 7

    Step 7: Dowels into the side rails

    Do the same at the other three rail ends.

    1:28
  8. 8

    Step 8: Dowels into the side rails

    Do the same at the other three rail ends.

    1:28
  9. 9

    Step 9: Dowels into the side rails

    Do the same at the other three rail ends.

    1:28
  10. 10

    Step 10: Cam locks into the side rails

    Push the eight cam locks into the round holes at the ends of the side rails.

    Note: The arrow on each lock must point to the end of the rail.

    2:09
  11. 11

    Step 11: Join the frame

    Push the side rails, the headboard and the footboard together.

    2:19
  12. 12

    Step 12: Tighten the cam locks

    Turn each cam lock clockwise with the Allen key until it is tight.

    Allen key
    2:29
  13. 13

    Step 13: Long dowels into the headboard

    Push the five long dowels into the top edge of the lower headboard.

    2:41
  14. 14

    Step 14: Bolts into the upper panel

    Lay the upper headboard panel on a table and screw the four hanger bolts into its bottom edge with the wrench.

    Wrench

    Safety: Stop turning when the collar of the bolt reaches the edge.

    2:51
  15. 15

    Step 15: Put on the upper panel

    Lower the upper panel onto the dowels. From the inside, push a plastic clip over each bolt and tighten a nut with the wrench.

    Wrench
    3:05
  16. 16

    Step 16: Screw on the slat rails

    Screw a steel slat rail to the inside of each side rail with eight screws.

    Phillips screwdriver

    Safety: Use the long wood screws here, not the short machine screws.

    Note: The rails can go higher or lower, to suit the thickness of your mattress.

    3:28
  17. 17

    Step 17: Middle beam brackets

    Screw a middle beam bracket to the middle of the headboard with two screws.

    Phillips screwdriver

    Safety: Use the long wood screws here, not the short machine screws.

    Note: Use the holes that match the height you chose for the slat rails.

    4:09
  18. 18

    Step 18: Middle beam brackets

    Screw the second bracket to the middle of the footboard the same way.

    Phillips screwdriver
    4:09
  19. 19

    Step 19: Diagonal braces

    Pull each diagonal brace out to length and screw it on under the bracket and under the slat rail.

    Phillips screwdriver

    Note: The braces slide out to the length you need.

    4:30
  20. 20

    Step 20: Square the frame

    Check that the frame is square, then fix each brace with one more screw through its slot.

    Phillips screwdriver

    Note: The sides must be straight, not bowed in or out.

    4:54
  21. 21

    Step 21: Put in the middle beam

    Lower the middle beam, still pushed together, with its head end into the bracket at the headboard.

    Safety: Do not hold your hand under the end of the beam: the edge is sharp.

    5:06
  22. 22

    Step 22: Put in the middle beam

    Pull the inner section out by its end hook and lower it into the bracket at the footboard.

    Note: Press down on the beam with your other hand while you pull.

    5:06
  23. 23

    Step 23: Slat holders

    Screw four slat holders onto the slat rails and the middle beam at the head end.

    Phillips screwdriver

    Note: Only needed for the roll-out LURÖY slats, not for a slatted base with a frame.

    5:26
  24. 24

    Step 24: Slat holders

    Screw the other four slat holders on at the foot end the same way.

    Phillips screwdriver
    5:26
  25. 25

    Step 25: Lay in the slatted bases

    Roll out the two slatted bases, then push the end slats inwards under the slat holders.

    5:54
