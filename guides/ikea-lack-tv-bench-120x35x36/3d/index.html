Steps

  1. 1Push a wooden dowel into one of the two holes on top of each leg.
  2. 2Set the shelf on the legs and fix each leg with a long screw and the Allen key.
  3. 3Screw the cam bolts into the top
  4. 4Lower the two side panels onto the cam bolts at each end.
  5. 5Push a cam lock into each round hole and turn it with a flat screwdriver to lock the sides.
  6. 6Screw an angle bracket to the top at the back edge, next to each side, with two screws each.
  7. 7Turn the shelf and legs over onto the sides and fix them with four long screws.
  8. 8Mark the drill holes on the wall
  9. 9Drive the wall screws
  10. 10Fix the bench to the wall
  11. ✓Finished

You need: Allen key 100092, Phillips screwdriver, Flat screwdriver, Pencil, Soft surface to work on (rug or blanket), Drill for the wall holes (not shown in the manual)
