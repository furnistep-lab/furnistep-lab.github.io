How to assemble the IKEA LACK TV bench 120x35x36 cm

Tools you need

  • Allen key 100092In the box
  • Phillips screwdriver
  • Flat screwdriver
  • Pencil
  • Soft surface to work on (rug or blanket)
  • Drill for the wall holes (not shown in the manual)

What's in the box

Parts

  • 1×Top panel
  • 2×Side panel
  • 1×Bottom shelf
  • 4×Leg

Hardware

  • 8×Long screw, hex socket
  • 4×Cam bolt
  • 4×Cam lock
  • 4×Short screw for the angle bracket
  • 4×Wooden dowel
  • 2×Angle bracket for wall fixing
  • 2×Bracket cover
  • 2×Keyhole plate
  • 2×Round-head wall screwNot included
  • 2×Wall plugNot included

Step by step

  1. 1

    Step 1: Push a wooden dowel into one of the two holes on top of each leg.

    3D view of step 1
  2. 2

    Step 2: Set the shelf on the legs and fix each leg with a long screw and the Allen key.

    Allen key 100092
    3D view of step 2
  3. 3

    Step 3: Screw the cam bolts into the top

    Lay the top face down on a soft surface and screw the four cam bolts into it.

    Soft surface to work on (rug or blanket)Phillips screwdriver
    3D view of step 3
  4. 4

    Step 4: Lower the two side panels onto the cam bolts at each end.

    3D view of step 4
  5. 5

    Step 5: Push a cam lock into each round hole and turn it with a flat screwdriver to lock the sides.

    Flat screwdriver
    3D view of step 5
  6. 6

    Step 6: Screw an angle bracket to the top at the back edge, next to each side, with two screws each.

    Phillips screwdriver
    3D view of step 6
  7. 7

    Step 7: Turn the shelf and legs over onto the sides and fix them with four long screws.

    Allen key 100092
    3D view of step 7
  8. 8

    Step 8: Mark the drill holes on the wall

    Stand the bench upright against the wall and mark through both bracket holes with a pencil.

    Pencil

    Safety: Always fix the bench to the wall: with a TV on it, it can tip over if a child climbs on it.

    3D view of step 8
  9. 9

    Step 9: Drive the wall screws

    Move the bench away and drive a round-head screw into each mark, leaving the head out.

    Drill for the wall holes (not shown in the manual)Flat screwdriver

    Note: Wall screws and plugs are not included. Use round-head screws that suit your wall, not countersunk ones.

    3D view of step 9
  10. 10

    Step 10: Fix the bench to the wall

    Push the bench back, hang a keyhole plate on each screw, tighten it and slide on the cover.

    Flat screwdriver
    3D view of step 10
