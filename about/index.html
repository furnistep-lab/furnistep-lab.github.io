About FurniStep

We make furniture assembly calm. Every FurniStep guide turns a flat-pack manual into a clear 3D animation you can follow at your own pace.

Why we started

Paper manuals are drawn without words, from angles that are not always easy to read. Video guides often mean fast cuts, talking and a camera that never quite shows the hole you need. We wanted something in between: the clarity of a drawing, with movement, and nothing to distract you.

How a guide is made

  1. Read the manual. Each step, part and screw is taken from the official assembly instructions for that exact product and size.
  2. Build it in 3D. We model the furniture and its hardware ourselves and animate every step, one part at a time.
  3. Check it. A person reviews the steps against the manual before anything is published. Where the manual is unclear, we say so instead of guessing.
  4. Publish. New guides go live on YouTube every Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday at 15:00 UTC, with chapters for every step and subtitles in nine languages.

What you can expect

  • No voiceover, ever. Just calm background music and the step on screen.
  • The tools you need and everything in the box, shown before the first step.
  • Safety steps, such as fixing furniture to the wall, always included.
  • Our own 3D models, animations and music. No manufacturer logos or artwork.
