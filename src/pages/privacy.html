Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026 (embedded videos added)

FurniStep ("we") publishes furniture assembly videos on YouTube at youtube.com/@FurniStep. This policy explains what data our website and our internal publishing tool handle.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or forms, and we do not collect personal data through it. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Guide pages can show our videos with an embedded YouTube player. The player is not loaded until you press play, so nothing is sent to YouTube when you only open a page. When you press play, the video loads from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), and YouTube processes your data under the Google Privacy Policy. Links to our channel and email open YouTube or your email app.

Our YouTube publishing tool and Google user data

We use an internal application, registered with Google as "FurniStep", only to manage our own YouTube channel. It is used solely by the channel owner and is not offered to the public.

  • What it accesses: with the channel owner's consent, the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API for the FurniStep channel: uploading and editing our own videos, captions, thumbnails and playlists, updating channel settings, and reading our channel's statistics.
  • How it is used: only to publish and manage FurniStep videos and to report on the channel's performance to the channel owner.
  • Sharing: we do not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Storage and security: the access credentials are stored encrypted in our private cloud project and are never published. Channel statistics are kept only as internal reports.
  • Revoking access: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Viewers and commenters

When you watch or comment on our videos, YouTube processes your data under its own policies. We only see what YouTube shows any channel owner, such as public comments and aggregated, anonymous statistics.
